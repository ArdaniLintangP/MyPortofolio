Smart Aquaculture Based on AIoT

KategoriMachine Learning
PeranMachine Learning Developer (Decision Tree Model Development)
Tahun2026

Project Overview

Smart Aquaculture is an AIoT-based monitoring and automation system designed to maintain optimal water quality in ornamental fish aquariums. The system collects environmental data such as temperature, pH, turbidity, and feeding conditions, then applies a Decision Tree machine learning model to predict water quality status and support automated device control. By combining IoT technology with machine learning, the system enables real-time monitoring, predictive analysis, and intelligent decision-making for aquarium management.

My Contribution

My primary responsibility in this project was the development and evaluation of the machine learning model used for water quality prediction. Key contributions included:
• Data preprocessing and preparation.
• Feature selection and analysis.
• Development of a Decision Tree classification model.
• Model training, testing, and performance evaluation.
• Assisting with the integration of prediction results into the overall AIoT system.

Challanges & Solutions

Water quality parameters can fluctuate significantly due to environmental changes, making it difficult to maintain stable aquarium conditions. A Decision Tree model was developed to classify water quality conditions based on sensor data, enabling automated monitoring and faster decision-making.

Dataset Features

The machine learning model utilizes several environmental parameters collected from the aquarium system:
• Water Temperature.
• pH Level.
• Water Turbidity.
• Feeding Status.

Results

The Decision Tree model achieved an average cross-validation accuracy of approximately 83%, demonstrating reliable performance in classifying aquarium water quality conditions.
